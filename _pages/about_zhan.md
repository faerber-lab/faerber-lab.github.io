Zhan Qu's research interests lie in explainable AI, machine learning, natural language processing, and graph neural networks. His recent work spans counterfactual and interpretable methods for dynamic graphs, explainable machine learning for aviation safety, and large language models for structured and temporally evolving data. He has published at venues such as ICML, KDD, AAAI, and AACL.
