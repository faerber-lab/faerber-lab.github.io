## Research Opportunities at Our Lab

We welcome outstanding researchers who are eager to work with us on-site. If you bring your own funding, feel free to ask Prof. Michael Färber if he would be interested in hosting you. 
If you don't have funding yet but need support, we outline the following fellowships and research awards -- several previous research stays in our group have been funded through these or similar programs. Please note that other fellowship opportunities are also available. We look forward to welcoming you to our lab!

### Humboldt Research Fellowship
- **Who's Eligible?**  
  Outstanding researchers at all career stages (**postdoc or experienced**) from any discipline or country.
- **What’s Offered?**  
  A monthly fellowship of €2,700 (postdocs) or €3,200 (experienced researchers) for 6–24 months (postdocs) or 6–18 months (experienced researchers). Additional benefits include family allowances, travel expenses, and language courses.
- **When to Apply?**  
  Applications are accepted on a rolling basis. Postdocs should apply within 4 years of earning their doctorate, while experienced researchers are eligible within 12 years. Selection is based on scientific excellence, the originality of the proposed project, and future potential.

### Research Awards
- **Examples:** Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel, Humboldt, and Georg Forster Research Prizes.
- **Who’s Eligible?**  
  Established, internationally recognized scientists with an extensive publication record and significant research achievements.
- **What’s Offered?**  
  Award funds (up to €60,000) for research stays at our hosting lab in Germany.

### Georg Forster Research Fellowship
- **Who's Eligible?**  
  Researchers from **developing or emerging countries** seeking to work on projects with a significant societal impact in their home country.
- **What’s Offered?**  
  Funding similar to the Humboldt Research Fellowship, including a monthly stipend of €2,700 or €3,200 depending on your career stage.
- **Why It Stands Out:**  
  This fellowship is specifically geared toward research that can benefit societal development in your home country.
