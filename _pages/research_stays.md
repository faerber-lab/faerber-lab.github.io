We welcome researchers eager to work with us on-site. If you already have your own funding, feel free to contact Prof. Michael Färber to discuss hosting possibilities.  
If you need external funding, below are several fellowships and research awards—many past research stays in our group have been supported through these or similar programs. Please note that additional fellowship opportunities may also be available. We look forward to welcoming you to our lab!

### Humboldt Research Fellowship
- **Who’s Eligible?**  
  Outstanding researchers at all career stages (**postdoc or experienced**) from any discipline or country.
- **What’s Offered?**  
  A monthly fellowship of €2,700 (for postdocs) or €3,200 (for experienced researchers) for 6–24 months (postdocs) or 6–18 months (experienced). Additional benefits include family allowances, travel expenses, and language courses.
- **When to Apply?**  
  Applications are accepted on a rolling basis. Postdocs should apply within 4 years of completing their doctorate; experienced researchers are eligible within 12 years. Selection is based on scientific excellence, the originality of the proposed project, and future potential.

### Research Awards
- **Examples:**  
  Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel, Humboldt, and Georg Forster Research Prizes
- **Who’s Eligible?**  
  Established, internationally recognized scientists with an extensive publication record and significant research achievements.
- **What’s Offered?**  
  Award funds (up to €60,000) for research stays at our hosting lab in Germany.

### Georg Forster Research Fellowship
- **Who’s Eligible?**  
  Researchers from **developing or emerging countries** working on projects with a strong societal impact in their home country.
- **What’s Offered?**  
  Funding similar to the Humboldt Research Fellowship, including a monthly stipend of €2,700 or €3,200 depending on your career stage.
- **Why It Stands Out:**  
  This fellowship is specifically tailored to research that can drive societal development in the applicant's home country.
