Four papers has been published at ACL 2026:

- [MediEval: A Unified Medical Benchmark for Patient-Contextual and Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning in LLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.734/) — Zhan Qu and Michael Färber. **Outstanding Paper Award.**
- [Attribution, Citation, and Quotation: A Survey of Evidence-based Text Generation with Large Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1430/) — Tobias Schreieder, Tim Schopf, and Michael Färber.
- [Quantifying the Impact of Translation Errors on Multilingual LLM Evaluation](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1916/) — Klaudia Thellmann, Bernhard Stadler, Michael Färber, and Jens Lehmann.
- [Tracing Relational Knowledge Recall in Large Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2160/) — Nicholas Popovič and Michael Färber.
