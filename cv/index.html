Summary Since April 2024, I have been W3 Full Professor at TU Dresden and the AI Center ScaDS.AI. My research focuses on natural language processing, knowledge representation, and machine learning, including neurosymbolic and explainable AI. I have authored over 100 peer-reviewed publications and secured various third-party funding as a Principal Investigator. Since 2019, I have taught lectures to 600 students and introduced the course “Behind the Secrets of Large Language Models.” Additionally, my B.A. in philosophy supports my interdisciplinary approach.
