Michael Färber's Research Group

ScaDS.AI, TU Dresden 📧 michael.faerber@tu-dresden.de.

Welcome! I’m Michael Färber, Full Professor and head of the Cognitive AI Unit at ScaDS.AI, TU Dresden, Germany. We focus on AI research that prioritizes trustworthiness and alignment with human values. Particularly, our research lies at the intersection of natural language processing (e.g., LLMs), graph-based machine learning, and structured knowledge representation (e.g., knowledge graphs). Check out our latest publications to see what we’ve been working on. For collaboration, feel free to reach out via email 📧
